Child Adoption Rules & Guidelines

1. Eligibility Requirements

Prospective adoptive parents must be at least 25 years of age and at least 21 years older than the child being adopted. Both married couples and single individuals may apply.

2. Background Verification

All applicants must undergo comprehensive background checks including criminal history, child abuse registry checks, and reference verification.

3. Home Study Requirement

A licensed social worker will conduct a thorough home study to assess the suitability of your home environment, living conditions, and financial stability.

4. Documentation

Applicants must provide valid identification, proof of income, medical certificates, marriage certificate (if applicable), and any other required documents.

5. Waiting Period

There is a mandatory waiting period of at least 6 months from application to placement for proper assessment and matching.

6. Post-Adoption Commitment

Adoptive parents agree to post-placement visits and follow-up assessments for a minimum of 2 years.

  • You must attend all mandatory pre-adoption counseling sessions.
  • You agree to maintain confidentiality regarding the child's biological family.
  • You understand that the child's welfare is the paramount consideration.
  • You agree to respect the child's cultural, religious, and ethnic heritage.
  • You acknowledge that providing false information is grounds for rejection.
  • You understand that adoption is irrevocable once finalized by the court.
  • You agree to notify the agency of any significant changes during the process.
  • You commit to ensuring the child has access to education and healthcare.
  • Parents must be physically, mentally, emotionally, and financially fit.
  • Adoption is legal only after court approval; private or direct adoption is prohibited.
  • All applicants must undergo background checks and a home study assessment.
  • Parents must submit all required documents, including ID, income proof, and medical certificates.
  • There is a mandatory waiting period before child placement to allow proper assessment and matching.
  • Parents must attend pre-adoption counseling sessions.
  • Adopted children must be treated equally to biological children, with access to education, healthcare, and a safe environment.
  • Parents must maintain confidentiality, notify the agency of significant changes, and understand that adoption is irrevocable once finalized by the court.
Legal Notice: By accepting these terms, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with all rules outlined above. Violation may result in termination of your application.
